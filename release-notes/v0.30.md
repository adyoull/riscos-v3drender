# V3DRender 0.30

- The GPU buffer area gets the size asked for (256 MB by default) even where
  RISC OS 5.30 would clamp it to 128 MB. V3DRender lifts the clamp just for
  its own area. `*V3DRender` says if it got less.
- No fixed limit of 4,096 buffers a program and 8,192 in all: the tables grow
  to 65,535. Doom 3 needs about 5,300.
- Buffers refused, and why, are counted in `*V3DRender` and noted in its log.

Thanks to David Sharp for finding both with his Quake 3, Mupen64Plus and Doom 3
ports.

Everything in 0.29 is included.
