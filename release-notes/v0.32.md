# V3DRender 0.32

- `DRM_IOCTL_RISCOS_PRESENT` now gets red and blue right in 64K-colour (5:6:5) modes. In 0.31 they were swapped there.
- 16 million and 32K colour modes were already right and are unchanged.

Thanks to MESA's first test of PRESENT on a Pi 4: 565 frames a second for a window, against 66 with the CPU plot.

Everything in 0.31 is included.
