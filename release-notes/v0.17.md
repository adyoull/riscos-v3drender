# V3DRender 0.17

The desktop's copies and fills on the GPU: window moves, scrolls,
`OS_Plot` block copies and solid rectangle fills (GraphicsV 13 ops 1
and 2), written again for V3DRender after Rob Heaton's V3DDriver. They
go through V3DRender's own queues, so they never meet a program's 3D
work on the hardware.

**Off by default.** Turn on with `*V3DRender 2D on` (each copy waited
for) or `*V3DRender 2D async` (not waited for, experimental), at load
with the `2D` option, or with `*Set V3DRender$2D on` before it loads.

- Rob's measured limits: small copies and fills stay on the CPU, which
  is quicker there.
- Left to the CPU: patterned fills, a cached screen, `OS_ScreenMode 4`'s
  "no hardware acceleration", and anything while a program has GPU work
  queued or running.
- `*V3DRender` shows what the GPU did and why the rest wasn't.
- Every program's submits are a little cheaper: a job's BO list of up
  to 48 is kept in the job, with no RMA claim.

Kit 20 adds Desk2D, which checks the GPU's copies and fills against the
CPU's on the screen and times both.

Not yet run on a Pi: please run kit 20's Check and Desk2D first.
