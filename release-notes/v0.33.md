# V3DRender 0.33

- For machines that stop at boot with V3DRender loading, such as a CM4:
  - `V3DRender$Options` gives the diagnostic options when RISC3D loads V3DRender at start-up.
  - The start-up log (`V3DRender$Log`) now carries on past "ready", noting the first of each later event.
- Nothing else changes.

Everything in 0.32 is included.
