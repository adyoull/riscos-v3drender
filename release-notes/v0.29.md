# V3DRender 0.29

- Configure's RISC3D window shows whether "Z-layer complex overlays" are on:
  video overlays (VideoOverlay) in layers behind the desktop, under its
  windows and menus, as display planes work on Linux. That needs
  `framebuffer_ignore_alpha=0` in config.txt and a 16 million colour alpha
  desktop mode (for example `C16M LABGR`). The line also says what is
  missing when it's off.

Everything in 0.28 is included.
