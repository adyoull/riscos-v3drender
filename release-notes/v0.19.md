# V3DRender 0.19

Desktop 2D measured on a Pi 4 (native RISC OS 5, 1920x1200, 32bpp):

- Correct on hardware: Desk2D's copies and fills match the CPU's, both
  waited for and not.
- V3DRender's own cost is about 5 us an op; a GPU job costs about 5 us
  plus 4 us a 64x64 tile. Big copies and fills are memory-bound (GPU
  1.3-2x the CPU); not waited for, the caller pays only 4-7 us.
- New default limits from the measurements: copies from 1 KB and 8
  rows, fills from 4 KB and 16 rows (Rob's V3DDriver: 2 KB/16 rows,
  16 KB/32 rows and a rule per tile).
- `*V3DRender` shows each op's time (list, submit, wait);
  `*V3DRender 2D zero` resets the counters.

Kit 22 adds Time2D, the timing test used for the limits.
